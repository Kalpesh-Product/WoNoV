# Welcome to <span className="wono-blue">WoNo</span>

Whether you're booking your first meeting room or coordinating work across departments, this documentation will help you get started. WoNoV is a workspace operations platform for the everyday work of running a shared space, from meeting bookings and visitor check-ins to billing and team performance.

## How work moves through WoNoV

A meeting booking is a good example. The person making the booking chooses a location, room, and available time. The operations team can then use the booking to prepare the room and update the meeting status. If the booking uses more credits than the client has available, the client’s meeting history shows the extra credits. Once payment is recorded, finance can review and verify it before uploading the invoice.

Different teams use WoNoV for different parts of the day. Front-desk staff handle visitor check-ins and refer to visit history. Employees and managers use **KRA and KPA** workflows to set work expectations, approve KPAs, schedule tasks, and review progress. In each case, knowing where to find the current status helps you see what’s done and who takes the next step.

## Your first visit

Start with a task you already know. If you’re booking a room, have your location, date, and preferred time ready. If you’re reviewing a payment, start with the client and the relevant date range so you can find the right record. The screens and actions available to you depend on your role and permissions. If a guide shows an action you don’t see, check with your WoNoV administrator about access.

Use the sidebar when you’re ready to try a task. The guides walk through the relevant screens and show what to check before submitting, approving, or completing a step.
