# KRA & KPA Userflow

Last updated: 27 November 2026

This workflow covers creating and approving KPAs, scheduling and completing tasks, reviewing performance, and managing self KRAs in BIZ Nest.

### KPA Creation and Approval

Employees create KPAs, which require manager approval before work can begin.

#### Employee

![](./images/media/image41.png)

1. Login to the employee account.

   ![](./images/media/image17.png)

2. Click on the KRA & KPA Module and then click on **Individual** card.

   ![](./images/media/image30.png)

3. Click on **Self KPA** tab to view the KPAs.

   ![](./images/media/image4.png)

4. Click on **Add Self KPA** to create a KPA.

:::note

(KPAs cannot be completed or used to create KPA Tasks before Manager’s approval)

:::

#### Manager

![](./images/media/image5.png)

1. Login to Manager Account.

   ![](./images/media/image37.png)

2. Click on the KRA & KPA Module and click on the **Individual** card.

   ![](./images/media/image43.png)

:::note

Managers can use these tabs to add their own KPAs/KRAs and schedule the same.

:::

![](./images/media/image10.png)

3. Click on the **Department Monthly KPA** Card present in the dashboard.

   ![](./images/media/image28.png)

4. Click on a Department to select a particular Department. Top Management/HR and Managers with multiple departments will have access to the department page and Managers with one department will redirected to Members page as below.

### Task Scheduling

Approved KPAs are divided into tasks, assigned deadlines, and scheduled through Daily Logs.

![](./images/media/image35.png)

1. Click on **Due Task** to view the unscheduled KPA tasks.

   ![](./images/media/image34.png)

2. Click on the \[Month\] KPA field to select a main KPA.

   ![](./images/media/image2.png)

3. Fill the rest of the fields to create a due KPA task.

   ![](./images/media/image21.png)

4. Select the KPA tasks to be scheduled and click on the **Add to Daily logs** button.

   ![](./images/media/image26.png)

5. Select a date to schedule the KPA tasks. You can also remove the selected tasks by clicking on the cross mark.

:::note

The Due tasks only contain unscheduled KPA tasks.The available deadline for the selected KPA Tasks is the nearest deadline among them.

:::

![](./images/media/image11.png)

:::note

Hence, few dates are enabled to be selected.

:::

![](./images/media/image29.png)

:::note

Employee can edit the due task but deadline can be edited only by Manager/HR/Top Management

:::

### Daily Task Management

Employees track task progress, complete assignments, and manage pending or carry-forward tasks.

![](./images/media/image63.png)

1. Click on the **Daily logs** to view the scheduled KPA Tasks.

:::note

Although Main KPA is listed, it can be marked as done from the Self KPA once the KPA tasks are completed.

:::

:::note

Any Pending task is fetched on the following day and marked as “Carry Forward”

:::

:::note

Newly Added Tasks are marked as “New Task”.

:::

![](./images/media/image69.png)

2. Click on the Add daily log button to add general tasks. Managers can also add new tasks to the dropdown.

   ![](./images/media/image54.png)

3. Click on the **Mark as done** button and fill the form to mark the KPA/General task as complete.

   ![](./images/media/image36.png)

4. Click on **mark as done** for a Carry forward task to mark it as done.

   ![](./images/media/image48.png)

:::note

Completed Daily logs are shown below the pending tasks in the same page.

:::

![](./images/media/image65.png)

5. Click on the history icon to check the edit history of the KPA task.

   ![](./images/media/image12.png)

6. Click on the **Self KPA** tab upon completing the KPA Tasks and mark the main KPA as done.

### Manager Review

Managers review completed KPAs and either close them or request changes.

![](./images/media/image5.png)

1. Login to Manager Account.

   ![](./images/media/image37.png)

2. Click on the KRA & KPA Module and click on the **Department Monthly KPA** Card.

   ![](./images/media/image28.png)

3. Click on a Department to select a particular Department. Top Management/HR and Managers with multiple departments will have access to the department page and Managers with one department will be redirected to Members page as below.

   ![](./images/media/image25.png)

4. Click on a particular member to view their dashboard.

   ![](./images/media/image9.png)

5. Click on the **Individual** Card to navigate to the employees KRA/KPA tasks.

   ![](./images/media/image27.png)

6. After review the task click on the edit icon to update review status.

   ![](./images/media/image23.png)

7. Update the review status. If the KPA requires any changes, select “**Changes Required**” or else “Closed”.

:::note

“Changes Required” status changes KPA status back to “Pending” and Review Status to “Pending” as well.

:::

#### Employee

![](./images/media/image41.png)

1. Login to the employee account.

   ![](./images/media/image17.png)

2. Click on the KRA & KPA Module and then click on **Individual** card.

   ![](./images/media/image51.png)

3. Click on the **Self KPA** tab.

   ![](./images/media/image18.png)

   ![](./images/incomplete-KPA-Tasks.png)

:::note

A KPA cannot be marked as complete until all KPA-Tasks have been completed.

:::

4. Click on **Mark as Done** button to mark the KPA as done for second review.

#### Manager

![](./images/media/image5.png)

1. Login to Manager Account.

   ![](./images/media/image37.png)

2. Click on the KRA & KPA Module and click on the **Individual** card.

   ![](./images/media/image10.png)

3. Click on the **Department Monthly KPA** Card present in the dashboard.

   ![](./images/media/image28.png)

4. Click on a Department to select a particular Department. Top Management/HR and Managers with multiple departments will have access to the department page and Managers with one department will be redirected to Members page as below.

   ![](./images/media/image25.png)

5. Click on a particular member to view their dashboard.

   ![](./images/media/image9.png)

6. Managers will be able to see the Employee Dashboard and have access to **Pending Approvals** tab.

   ![](./images/media/image44.png)

7. Select **Self KPA** and then click on edit icon to update the review status after reviewing the KPA.

   ![](./images/media/image38.png)

![](./images/media/image14.png)

### HR Review and Final Closure

HR provides ratings and comments, completing the final KPA closure process.

![](./images/media/image59.png)

1. Login to HR Account.

   ![](./images/media/image55.png)

2. Click on the KRA & KPA Module and click on **Individual** card.

   ![](./images/media/image43.png)

3. HR can use these tabs to add their own KPAs/KRAs and schedule the same.

   ![](./images/media/image13.png)

4. Click on the Department Card present in the dashboard to select a particular Dashboard. Top Management/HR and Managers with multiple departments will have access to the department page and Managers with one department will be redirected to Members page as below.

   ![](./images/media/image19.png)

5. Click on a particular member to view their dashboard.

   ![](./images/media/image16.png)

6. Click on the **Individual** Card.

   ![](./images/media/image53.png)

7. Select the **Self KPA** card and click on edit icon.

   ![](./images/media/image6.png)

8. Add the HR comment and suitable rating.

   ![](./images/media/image58.png)

:::note

Once the KPA is rated by HR the Final Closure status changes from “Pending” to “Closed” and then it is added in the Completed Self KPA table.

:::

![](./images/media/image45.png)

9. Click on the history icon to view the edit history of a particular KPA.

### Performance Tracking

KPA ratings, Daily Log ratings, and pending closures are available through dashboards.

![](./images/media/image1.png)

1. Go back to the Employee Dashboard and click on **Daily Score** to view the Ratings Tab.

   ![](./images/media/image49.png)

2. Click on **KPA Rating** to view the KPA task rating.

   ![](./images/media/image64.png)

:::note

Pending Closure indicates how many KPAs are yet to be rated A particular task is eligible for final closure % only when both Manager and HR has rated

:::

![](./images/media/image50.png)

3. Click on the **Daily Logs Rating** tab to view the **Daily logs rating**.

   ![](./images/media/image47.png)

4. Click on the number in the table to view the tasks’ details.

   ![](./images/media/image40.png)

:::note

This modal contains task details from the daily logs tab.

:::

![](./images/media/image42.png)

:::note

Similarly, Managers and HR can click on the Daily score card present on their Dashboard to view their ratings

:::
